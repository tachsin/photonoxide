## 1. How does heat shift a waveguide's phase?

::coming
The thermo-optic coefficient $dn/dT$, the power $P_\pi$ a heater needs for a phase of $\pi$, its
time constant, and the crosstalk to neighbouring guides.

## 2. How does an electric field change a crystal's index?

::coming
The Pockels effect: the electro-optic tensor rotated into the waveguide's frame for x-cut and
z-cut lithium niobate, and the index change by perturbation theory.

## 3. What does $V_\pi L$ measure, and why do papers quote it differently?

::coming
The voltage–length product for a phase of $\pi$, from the overlap of the optical mode and the
electrode field, and the one definition photonoxide derives and uses.

## 4. What limits a modulator's speed?

::coming
Travelling-wave electrodes: the RF index matched to the optical group index, the line's impedance
and loss, and the electro-optic frequency response that follows.

## 5. How does a Mach–Zehnder or a ring turn phase into intensity?

::coming
Push-pull arms biased at quadrature against a ring's resonance shifted by a small index change:
drive voltage, optical bandwidth and sensitivity to temperature.

## 6. How do free carriers modulate silicon?

::coming
The plasma dispersion effect, a p–n junction depleted by a reverse bias, and its capacitance, which
ROADMAP.md's 0.8 brings with drift-diffusion.
