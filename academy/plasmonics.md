## 1. What is a metal's permittivity at optical frequencies?

::coming
The Drude model of free electrons, the plasma frequency, and why the real permittivity is negative
below it.

## 2. What is a surface plasmon?

::coming
The TM wave bound to an interface between $\varepsilon_m < 0$ and $\varepsilon_d > 0$, its
dispersion relation, and why no TE wave is bound there.

## 3. How far does a surface plasmon travel, and how tightly is it held?

::coming
Its propagation length from the imaginary part of its wavevector, and its decay lengths into the
metal and the dielectric, from the complex permittivity.

## 4. What does Mie's series give?

::coming
A sphere's exact scattering, absorption and extinction, multipole by multipole, checked against
Mie's 1908 Table I.

## 5. What is a localized plasmon resonance?

::coming
A small metal sphere's dipole resonance at $\varepsilon_m = -2\varepsilon_d$, and how it shifts and
broadens as the sphere grows.

## 6. How does FDTD model a metal?

::coming lorentz_okoniewski
Drude and Lorentz terms by auxiliary differential equations, a Lorentz half-space's reflection
against the analytic one, and a sphere against Mie's series.
