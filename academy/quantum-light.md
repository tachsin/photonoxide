## 1. What happens when two photons meet at a coupler?

::coming
Hong–Ou–Mandel interference: indistinguishable photons leave a 50:50 coupler together, and the dip
in coincidences against their delay and spectra.

## 2. How does a programmable interferometer make any unitary?

::coming
Meshes of Mach–Zehnder interferometers and phase shifters, and the Reck and Clements
decompositions of an $N \times N$ unitary into them.

## 3. Why are photon statistics hard to compute?

::coming
The probability of an output pattern is the squared permanent of a submatrix of the circuit's
unitary, Ryser's and Glynn's formulas for it, and boson sampling.

## 4. What does loss do to a quantum circuit?

::coming
A lossy circuit dilated to a unitary with noise modes, and loss budgets per path, per photon and
per event from each component's S-matrix.

## 5. How good is a heralded single-photon source?

::coming
A photon pair's purity, heralding efficiency through the filters, and multi-pair probability, from
the joint spectral amplitude of nonlinear optics.

## 6. What is squeezed light, and what is Gaussian boson sampling?

::coming
Squeezed states from parametric amplifiers, Gaussian states described by covariance matrices, and
pattern probabilities as hafnians.

## 7. How could photons compute?

::coming
From the KLM scheme to fusion-based architectures, GKP states and cluster states: what each needs
from sources, circuits and detectors.
