## 1. What does a link need?

::coming
A source, a modulator, a channel, a detector and a receiver, the power budget through them, and
the bit error rate it leaves at a data rate.

## 2. How does multiplexing multiply the bandwidth?

::coming
Many wavelengths on one fibre, ring modulators and filters one per channel, and combs that supply
all the wavelengths at once, as Rizzo et al.'s Kerr comb-driven link does.

## 3. What does each bit cost?

::coming
Energy per bit from the laser, the modulator's driver and the receiver, and how 3D integration of
photonics with electronics brings it to tens of femtojoules, as Daudlin et al.'s does.

## 4. What is co-packaged optics?

::coming
Optics moved from the front panel into the processor's package, the fibres' attachment, and the
co-design of photonics with CMOS that Shekhar et al. and Wan et al. set out.
