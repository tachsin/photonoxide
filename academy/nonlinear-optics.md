## 1. Which materials can carry a nonlinear process at given wavelengths?

::coming
Each crystal's transparency window: its band-gap edge, its multiphonon edge, and where two-photon
absorption sets in below twice the band-gap wavelength.

## 2. What are $\chi^{(2)}$ and $\chi^{(3)}$, and how large are they?

::coming
The $d$ tensor on Shoji's scale dispersed by Miller's rule, and $\chi^{(3)}$ and $n_2$, with their
sources.

## 3. What is phase matching, and how is it reached in a waveguide?

::coming
The mismatch $\Delta k$ of a three- or four-wave process, birefringent, modal and quasi-phase
matching by periodic poling, and the acceptance bandwidths in wavelength and temperature.

## 4. How efficient is second-harmonic generation in a waveguide?

::coming
Coupled-mode equations with overlaps from the mode solver's full-vector fields: the normalized
efficiency in %/W/cm², the undepleted sinc² and the depleted tanh².

## 5. What do four-wave mixing and parametric gain need in silicon?

::coming
The nonlinear parameter $\gamma$ from the mode's overlap, the dispersion that sets the gain's band,
and two-photon and free-carrier absorption.

## 6. How does a ring make a Kerr comb?

::coming
Resonant enhancement in a ring, the Lugiato–Lefever equation solved by split-step with the mode
solver's dispersion, and dissipative solitons.

## 7. How are photon pairs made, and how pure are they?

::coming
Spontaneous parametric down-conversion and four-wave mixing, the joint spectral amplitude from the
phase matching and the pump, and its purity by Schmidt decomposition.
