## 1. How does a grating couple a guide to a fibre?

::coming
The first-order phase-matching condition $n_\mathrm{eff} - n_c \sin\theta = \lambda/\Lambda$ between
the guided mode, the grating's period $\Lambda$ and the angle $\theta$ in a cladding of index $n_c$.

## 2. What sets the coupling strength and the beam's shape?

::coming
The etch depth and fill factor set the decay length, and the exponential beam's overlap with a
fibre's Gaussian mode sets the coupling efficiency.

## 3. Why is a grating coupler's bandwidth limited?

::coming
The angle that phase matching gives changes with wavelength, so the beam walks off the fibre's
fixed angle; the bandwidth follows from the group index and the beam's width.

## 4. Where does the rest of the light go?

::coming
Down into the substrate, back into the guide, and through the grating's end; how the buried
oxide's thickness, a bottom mirror and apodization recover it.

## 5. How is a grating coupler computed, and what does a 2D result mean?

::coming
The cross-section along the guide in 2D FDFD or FDTD, the fibre's mode overlapped with the field
above, and why that is a design estimate, not the 3D device's efficiency.
