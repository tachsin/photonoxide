Setup
  crystal: congruent LiNbO3, Edwards and Lawrence's n_o(T) and n_e(T); lithium-rich, Jundt et al.'s, for dT at 0.86 um (footnote f)
  device: 1 mm, first-order QPM at a 50 % duty cycle, 25 °C
  thermal expansion: Jundt's along the grating, 1.54e-5 /K and 5.3e-9 /K^2
  processes: SHG of 0.86, 1.064 and 2.12 um: d31 (o + o -> e), d33 (e + e -> e), d22 (o + o -> o)
  solver: the mismatch from the indices; Fejer's linear estimate Eq. (25), the noncritical Eq. (41), Eq. (A1.9), or the half-maximum points found numerically
  tolerance: half a unit of each value's last printed digit
  materials:
    LiNbO3: Edwards & Lawrence 1984: edwards-lawrence-1984
    LiNbO3, lithium-rich: Jundt, Fejer & Byer 1990: jundt-1990
