Setup
  device: a 2.8 × 2.8 µm region of 220 nm silicon on silica, air above; an input and two outputs 500 nm wide, the outputs' centres 0.54 µm above and below the input's axis (Fig. 1(b))
  wavelengths: 1300 nm to port 2 (above) and 1550 nm to port 3 (below), each with four more at ±1 and ±2 % of its frequency; the input's fundamental TE mode
  2D: the effective index method: the 220 nm slab's TE index in the silicon and air's outside, H along z; 20 nm cells, 10 nm beside; PMLs of 0.3 µm
  design: 140 × 140 pixels of 20 nm, a 100 nm conic filter, tanh projection at η = ½, β 8 to 256 in six stages of 40 evaluations; genoxide's MMA on the worst of the ten 1 − T and of the ten shares into the other port times 10
  3D (--full): the design extruded through the layer; cells of 36.7, 31.4 and 27.5 nm, stretched PMLs of 16 cells, GMRES and the multigrid to 1e-9
  tolerance: 2D: their specification (T ≥ 90 %, the other port ≤ 1 %) reported beside ours; 3D: our loss and our other port's share at most Fig. 4(a)'s, read to ±0.1 and ±0.5 dB, where our grids' spread decides it
