Setup
  domain: 350 × 300 cells of 10 nm (the testbed's model), 20 cells of PML; a 1.6 × 1.6 µm design region from (0.95, 0.70) µm between two 400 nm waveguides 0.75 µm long
  wavelengths: 1265, 1270, 1275, 1285, 1290 and 1295 nm
  ports: 1.9 µm windows at x = 0.25 and 3.25 µm: the fundamental mode in on the left, the second mode out on the right; E along z
  our design: 160 × 160 densities, a conic filter of 50 nm, tanh projection at η = ½, β 8 to 256 in six stages of 30 evaluations, then 50 nm linewidth and spacing (Zhou et al., Arrieta et al.'s c = 64 R² and ε) for 60; genoxide's MMA on the epigraph of the worst R and 1 − T
  tolerance: the published transmissions to the README's printed digits (0.005 dB); our length within 20 % of 50 nm (Chen et al.); our R and T within the published range at 55 to 76 nm
  materials:
    silicon: ε 12.25
    oxide: ε 2.25
