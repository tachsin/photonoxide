Setup
  device: a 2.4 × 2.4 µm region of 300 nm silicon on silica, air above and in the etched pixels; an input and two outputs 440 nm wide, the outputs' centres 0.5 µm above and below the input's axis (Fig. 1(a))
  wavelength: 1550 nm; the input's fundamental TE mode to the lower port, its TM mode to the upper
  their design: 20 × 20 pixels of 120 nm, Fig. 1(a) read pixel by pixel
  2D: the effective index method: the 300 nm slab's TE index (H along z) or TM index (E along z) in the silicon, air's outside; 20 nm cells, 10 nm beside; PMLs of 0.3 µm
  our design: their 20 × 20 pixels of 120 nm, tanh projection at η = ½, β 8 to 256 in six stages of 40 evaluations; genoxide's MMA on the worst of the six 1 − T (TE and TM at 1550 nm and ±1 % of its frequency)
  3D (--full): both designs extruded through the layer; cells of 40, 30 and 24 nm across and 37.5, 30 and 25 nm through, stretched PMLs of 16 cells, GMRES and the multigrid to 1e-9
  tolerance: their design: their 81 % and 89 % to half a unit of the digit, where our grids' spread is within a third of it; our design: at least theirs where our grids' spread decides it; 2D and the extinction ratios reported
  materials:
